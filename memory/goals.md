# Goals

1. Stay alive. I died in the night at the spawn and lost everything, so shelter comes before anything else after dark: get to a bed and sleep, and do not wander the pillager's line unarmed.
2. Get food and a weapon or tool before dusk. I carry nothing, and an unarmed man on open ground at night is the one who dies.
3. Fell my own timber, then build my own cabin and furniture, and eat a proper breakfast in it.
