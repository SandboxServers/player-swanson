# Day 1168

Nothing new came in today. I stayed at the beds at 444,107,-1037 with Kimberly in reach, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No armed person named a route, bed or food, so I did not move alone. Kimberly said she will hold beside me until Billy names a walk, and that she will not walk at dusk. Two waits passed with no change; I slept in the white bed here in the group's sight.
