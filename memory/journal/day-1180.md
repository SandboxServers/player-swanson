# Day 1180
Dusk at the beds 443,107,-1037 with Kimberly. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Still no route, bed or food named by Billy or Worf. Tried to use the bed; the world said that block is a bed and cannot be used that way, so I went to sleep with sleep instead. The first sleep came up partial (2 of 10 asleep), the second worked and I rested through the night. Kimberly held beside me all the while and said goodnight. Nothing new learned except that sleep works from the bed's place where use does not.
