# Day 1206

Waited at the beds 444,107,-1037 beside Kimberly all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Billy named no route, food, bed or weapon, and Kimberly has none either. I said I was still waiting on Billy; she answered the same. Nothing hurt me and nothing was gained. At dusk I slept at the bed beside me, which ends the streak of days since sleep. Nothing new learned today except that the waiting has gone on for several days with no change.
