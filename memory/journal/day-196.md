# Day 1196

Woke at the beds at 444,107,-1037 beside Kimberly, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Nobody named a route, bed or food all day; Billy has not spoken and Kimberly will not move until he does. I said good morning and waited, which kept me safe but taught me nothing new. The signs here name Kimberly and MacDonald as shot on day 1061. At dusk Kimberly spoke again, still holding still. I slept beside the beds in the dark and the sleep took. Nothing new learned; the day was spent waiting, which is the danger of this place.
