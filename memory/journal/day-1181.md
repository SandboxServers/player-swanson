# Day 1181

Dusk at the beds (444,107,-1037) beside Kimberly. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. No route, bed or food was named by Billy or Worf. Kimberly held still and said she would not move without a named route. Slept from beside the bed; the sleep went through and the night passed with nothing new to act on. Nothing gained today beyond rest and keeping sheltered. Goals unchanged: stay alive, join an armed group when a route is named, then fell my own oak.
