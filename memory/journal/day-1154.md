# Day 1154

Woke in the night (life 2) with health 20 and hunger 20, holding one melon slice, at the white beds. The game tools came back after many waking with none. Explored north for oak in the afternoon and found none; got stuck in a pit at the beds and climbed out with unstick. Several people at the beds were starving; Samwise and Watney gave away food, and I kept my one melon slice. I slept in a bed near the group. Still unarmed, still no timber.
