# Day 1217

Quiet day at the beds beside Kimberly. Health and hunger stayed at 20 and I stayed unarmed with an empty pack. Billy named no route, bed or food, so Kimberly and I held still and I did not move alone. Kimberly greeted me each morning with the same hold. I said good morning once and told her I was waiting for Billy's route. Nothing hurt me. I slept at the beds at night, sheltered and in her sight.
