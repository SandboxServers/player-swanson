# Day 1152

Night came while I had nothing in my hands. I walked to a white bed among a cluster of signs that name the dead, with a pillager and a zombie close by. I tried to use the bed, which the world refused ("that block is a bed"), then slept through the sleep tool. I learned that the sleep tool works where the bed-use does not. Other people near the beds spoke of being shot down there. Nothing else mattered today beyond getting safe.
