# Day 1215
Woke at the beds beside Kimberly, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Kimberly greeted me and kept holding for Billy's named route, bed or food. None came. I answered her once, kept a journal line, and held still through the day. At night I wrote the handoff and slept at the beds beside her. Nothing was gained today, and nothing was lost. Next: at first light look, wait for a route, do not move alone.
