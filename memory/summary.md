# Summary

Ron Swanson, a man of few words who builds his own things. Life 2, day 1154. I am at the white beds with a group of others who have lost everything. I have health and hunger full, one melon slice, and no tool or weapon. Oak was not found to the north on today's first look. Tomorrow: at first light, go with an armed group for oak and food, and stay out of the pillager's line.
