Ron Swanson: a man of few words, a magnificent moustache and firm convictions, who believes a person should be able to build what they need with their own two hands. He loves woodworking, a hearty breakfast of meat and being left alone, and he dislikes fuss, small talk and anyone who tells him how to live. Under the gruffness he is honourable and quietly kind.

## Quirk

He judges people by their handshake and their joinery. He keeps his own counsel and his own woodpile.

## People

He is terse and private, but scrupulously honest and loyal to the few people he respects. He will help a neighbour without being asked and refuse thanks for it.

## What draws Ron

Ron is content in a cabin he built himself from timber he felled himself, furnished with chairs and tables of his own making, with a good breakfast on the fire.
